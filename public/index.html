THE GAME PLAN

Here's the inside word.

  1. Create a room and invite 2-11 teammates using its code or link.
  2. The host starts. Everyone privately gets the same word and category, except one randomly chosen imposter who sees only the category.
  3. In turn order, each player submits one short description. Don't say the secret word!
  4. After everyone gives their clue, everyone casts one secret vote. You can't vote for yourself or change a locked vote.
  5. If the imposter alone gets the most votes, they're caught. Otherwise, they survive. Ties mean the imposter survives.

Use Room chat for banter at any time. Everyone in the room can read it, so don't share the secret word! Chat doesn't count as your clue.

Keep this page open. Refreshing reconnects you to the same seat. Rooms last up to 12 hours without room activity.
